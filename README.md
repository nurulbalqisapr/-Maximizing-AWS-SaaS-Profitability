## Maximizing SaaS Profitability: Optimizing Discount Strategies to Improve Profit and Preserve Margins.
Amazon Web Service SaaS Sales Data Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes AWS SaaS sales data to understand how discount strategies impact profitability and profit margins. The main objective is to evaluate whether discounts truly drive business growth or instead reduce long-term profitability.

---

## Problem Statement

SaaS companies frequently use discounts to increase sales and attract customers. However, excessive discounting may reduce profit and weaken margins.

How can discount strategies be optimized to improve SaaS profitability without sacrificing profit margins?

---

## Objectives

1. Analyze the impact of discounts on profit and sales  
2. Evaluate the relationship between discount and profit margin  
3. Identify optimal discount levels across products and customers  
4. Understand customer behavior through segmentation  
5. Build a data-driven discount optimization strategy  

---

## Key Insight / Business Principle

Discounts should be treated as a strategic lever rather than a growth shortcut, and must be optimized based on customer value segmentation to maximize profitability and protect profit margins.

---

## Methodology

- Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering  
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
- Correlation Analysis  
- Linear Regression Modeling  
- RFM Customer Segmentation  
- Discount Optimization Simulation  

---

## Key Findings

- Discounts have a negative impact on profit  
- Increasing discounts does not significantly increase sales  
- Profitability varies strongly across customer segments  
- Uniform discount strategies are inefficient  
- Segment-based pricing improves profitability  

---

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that discounting is not an effective driver of sales growth but significantly reduces profitability. The optimal strategy is to apply controlled, segment-based discounts rather than uniform discounting.

---

## Business Recommendation

- Implement strict discount caps  
- Use RFM-based segmentation for pricing strategy  
- Reduce dependency on discounts for growth  
- Focus on profit margin as a core KPI  
- Apply higher control for high-value customers
